# Topical Authority — bambu.tukang.co.id

## Role and boundary

Bambu.tukang.co.id is a specialist education and commercial-support property for bamboo architecture, construction, landscape works, furnishings, inspection, and lifecycle care in Indonesia. It should help owners, designers, builders, craftspeople, and procurement teams define safe, durable bamboo work. Existing application and location pages retain transactional intent. Species, maturity, grade, moisture, treatment, structural loads, joints, foundations, fire/weather/biological resistance, durability, standards, certification, warranty, and prices require current project-appropriate evidence.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub and next decision. Fundamentals link to material quality and preservation; material pages link to structural and joint design; building/application pages link to foundations, envelope, and specification; fabrication links to handover; inspection links to maintenance, repair, replacement, or warranty. Commercial routes receive links only when scope, evidence, and risk ownership are clear.
